ABOUT

Asian creatives are driving global culture in film, theater, music, visual art, food, and design, and Asians in Arts brings their voices together to tell the full story of that creative force. Hosted by former CNN International anchor Karuna Shinsho and producer Szewah Chin — warm,funny and candid conversations with working artists and icons alike. New episode every two weeks.
